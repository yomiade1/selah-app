DESIGN REFERENCE FOR THE BUILD

USWDS + Apple hybrid

The same seven screens, content and states as the original prototype, rebuilt with a hybrid design language. It uses the U.S. Web Design System’s accessibility rules and plain-language components, inside Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines structure and materials. Planning and editing use the light appearance. Live, stage and pastor screens use the dark Booth appearance.

From USWDS

  • Colour tokens (primary #005ea2, state colours) at WCAG AA contrast or better
  • Alerts with a thick left border and an icon; plain-language headings
  • Uppercase status tags, a step indicator, summary boxes, a process list
  • Every form field has a label, and a hint where needed
  • A 4 px blue focus ring on every control; bordered tables
  • Hint text under every setting, and info buttons for longer help

From Apple HIG

  • Translucent toolbar and bottom bar
  • Sidebar source lists with rounded selection
  • Inset grouped lists, switches, segmented controls
  • Large titles with a small eyebrow label above
  • Cards with large rounded corners and soft shadows
  • Light and dark (Booth) appearances; iOS-style phone screens

Rules for combining them

  • USWDS decides meaning: colour, wording, states, accessibility
  • Apple decides structure and feel: layout, materials, motion
  • Buttons use USWDS colours and weights, with Apple’s rounded shape
  • Touch targets are at least 44 px, which both systems require
  • Type: Inter for interface text, Manrope for headings, 15 px base, with a text-size setting from 90% to 200%
  • No government branding: no .gov banner and no flag
1 · Live, Producer mode

Booth appearance. Source-list service order, a large title, an alert for the scripture detected in speech, and inset grouped layers.
2 · Live, Simple mode

A step indicator shows where you are in the service. Big rounded USWDS buttons and a success alert.
3 · Song editor

A four-step flow for adding a song, labelled fields with hints, and inset grouped switches.
4 · Service planner

A bordered USWDS table inside an Apple card, with a summary box for the “Ready for Sunday?” check.
5 · Screens and outputs

Sidebar of outputs, range sliders with hints, health in grouped lists.
6 · Stage display

Public Sans at stage size, a gold key badge, and the booth message as a USWDS warning alert.
8 · Settings, text size and contextual help

The text-size setting with a live preview, hint text, an info popover, and the offline help panel showing articles for this screen.
7 · Browser remote and pastor view

iOS-style phones, with a USWDS process list explaining how to pair.
