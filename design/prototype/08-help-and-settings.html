TASK

Change the text size

You can make the text in Selah bigger. This helps if the words are hard to read.

  1. Select Settings, then Appearance.
  2. Under Text size in Selah, pick a size.
  3. Check the preview. If it looks good, you are done.
i

This does not change the words on your screens. To change those, edit the template.
