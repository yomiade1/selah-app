# Selah couldn't do that

Sometimes Selah receives a request it can't finish. Your songs and services stay safe.

## Try these in order

1. Wait a moment and try again.
2. Close and open the window or panel you were using.
3. If the problem stays, save your work and restart Selah.
4. Select **Help**, then **Report a problem** to send us the log file.
