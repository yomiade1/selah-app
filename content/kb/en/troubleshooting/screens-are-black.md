# The screens are black

Try these in order. Most problems are fixed by step 1 or 2.

## Try these in order

1. Check if the screens were cleared. Look at **On screen now**. If it is empty, select a slide to show it.
2. Check that the projector or TV is on. Make sure it is set to the right input.
3. Check the cable. Unplug it at both ends, then plug it back in.
4. In Selah, go to **Screens**. Look for a red dot next to a screen. A red dot means Selah can't reach that screen.
5. Turn the projector or TV off, wait 10 seconds, then turn it back on.

## Still black?

- Select **Show test pattern**. If you can't see the pattern, the problem is the cable or the screen. It is not Selah.
- Ask the person who looks after your equipment.
- Or select **Help**, then **Report a problem**. Send the file to our support team.

> [!NOTE]
> Selah keeps showing the last picture even if the app closes. If the screens went black and the app is still open, the problem is likely the cable or the screen.
