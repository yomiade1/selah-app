# Setting is locked by your admin

Your church admin or computer policy set this option and locked it. Selah shows the locked value and keeps it in place.

## What to do

1. Ask your church tech lead or IT admin if you need this setting changed.
2. Open **Settings** if you want to check which level sets the value.
