# The words don't fit on a screen

Some words in a song are too big for one of your screens, even at the smallest text size the template allows.

## Try these in order

1. Open the song and split long lines into two.
2. Open the template and lower the smallest text size.
3. Pick another template for that item in the service.
4. Select **Check the service** again.
