# The sound is too loud

Selah has a volume limiter. It stops sudden loud sounds before they reach the speakers. It is always on.

## Try these in order

1. Turn down the volume on the sound desk, not only in Selah.
2. Clear the sound with **Clear everything**. Selah fades it out.
3. If Ready for Sunday says the limiter is off, select **Help**, then **Report a problem**.
