# Fix a flashing content warning

Selah checks clips and transitions for harsh flashes (over three flashes a second). Use slow fades and calm clips so screens stay safe.

## Try these in order

1. Make the fade longer or clear fast repeats in **Repeat cadence (ms)**.
2. Switch a fast loop to a soft fade or still look under **Background**.
3. Pick a softer clip in **Media** when a file shows a **Flash alert** badge.
4. Stage a flagged slide only after you check it safely and tick the warning box.

## Still not working?

- Press [[F12]] or select **Clear everything** at once if live screens look unsafe.
