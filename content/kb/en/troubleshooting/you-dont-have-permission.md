# You don't have permission

Selah stopped the change you asked for. This keeps the screens safe. Nothing on screen changed.

## Why it happens

- The booth is locked. Only Next, Previous, and Clear work. The booth team can unlock it.
- This account is locked to Simple mode. You can show slides and clear the screens. You can't edit songs or change settings.

## What to do

1. Keep going with **Next slide**, **Back**, and **Clear everything**. They always work.
2. If you need more, select **Message the booth** and ask the booth team.
