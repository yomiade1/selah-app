# Get help in Selah

Selah keeps every guide offline on your computer. You can open help next to your work without blocking your screens.

## Steps

1. Press [[F1]] or select **Help**, then **Help for this screen (F1)**.
2. Read the guides under **For this screen**, or type words in search and select **Search**.
3. To check one control, press [[Shift]]+[[F1]] or select **What's this? (Shift+F1)**.
4. Select any button or panel to read a short note and open its guide.
5. Press [[Esc]] or select **Done** when you finish checking controls.

> [!TIP]
> You can still press [[F12]] to clear the screens while help is open.

## If you can't find an answer

- Select **Back to topics** in the help panel and try shorter search words.
- Select **Help**, then **Report a problem** to send us a note.
