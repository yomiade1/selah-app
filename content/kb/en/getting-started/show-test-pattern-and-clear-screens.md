# Show a test pattern and clear screens

Before a service starts, you can test every connected screen. At any time, you can clear every screen in one move.

## Steps

1. Look at **On screen** in **Preview** to see what people see right now.
2. Select **Show test pattern** to send colour bars to every screen.
3. Check the screens in the room to make sure edges and colours look right.
4. Select **Clear everything** or press [[F12]] to clear every screen right away.

> [!NOTE]
> Pressing [[F12]] or selecting **Clear everything** always works, even if a search box is open.
