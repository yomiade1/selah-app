# Change appearance and text size

You can make Selah dark for a dim sound booth and make labels larger. Slides on your screens never change size.

## Steps

1. Select **Settings** in the top toolbar.
2. Under **Appearance**, pick **Light**, **Booth**, or **Automatic**.
3. Under **Text size**, drag the slider to grow or shrink Selah's text.
4. Turn on **Use my computer's text size** if you want Selah to match your system scale.

> [!TIP]
> Press [[Ctrl]]+[[+]] to grow text, [[Ctrl]]+[[−]] to shrink it, or [[Ctrl]]+[[0]] to reset it.
