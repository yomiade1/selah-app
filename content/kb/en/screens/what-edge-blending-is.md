# What edge blending is

Some rooms put 2 or more projectors side by side to make one wide picture. Where the pictures meet, you can often see a bright line or a gap.

Edge blending fixes this. The pictures overlap a little. In the overlap, Selah fades one picture out as the next one fades in. The line goes away. People see one smooth picture.

## An example

A church has 3 projectors above the stage. Together they make one long screen. Each picture overlaps the next one by about a fifth of its width. Selah blends each overlap, so the words can run across the whole screen.

## What you need

- Projectors that are the same model, or very close.
- Projectors that are lined up and in focus.
- A computer that can send a picture to all of them at the same time.
