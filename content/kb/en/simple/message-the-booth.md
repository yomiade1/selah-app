# Message the booth

Need help during the service? Send a note to the booth team.

## Steps

1. Select **Message the booth**.
2. Type a short note, like "Mic 2 is not working".
3. Select **Send to the booth**.

The booth team sees your note at the top of their screen. It never shows on the main screens.

> [!TIP]
> Keep it short. The booth team reads it while the service is going.
