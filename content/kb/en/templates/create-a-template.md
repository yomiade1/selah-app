# Create and style a template

Templates set the font, colour, card box, and safe margins for songs and scripture slides. Start from a built-in look or make your own.

## Steps

1. Open **Templates** in the main toolbar.
2. Select **New template** or pick a saved one to edit.
3. Pick a starter look such as **Sanctuary lyrics** or **Warm serif**.
4. Choose a **Text role**, then set its font, size, weight, outline, or card box.
5. Add screen shapes such as **16:9** or **LED 32:9** and check the safe margins.
6. Select **Save template** to keep your changes.

> [!TIP]
> Use **Smallest size (pt)** so long lines shrink cleanly before splitting into extra slides.
