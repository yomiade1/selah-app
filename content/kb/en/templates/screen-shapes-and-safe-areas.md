# Set screen shapes and safe area margins

Rooms often mix widescreen projectors, ultra-wide LED walls, and online streams. One template can adapt to each shape.

## Steps

1. Open **Templates** and select a saved style.
2. Under **Screen shapes**, pick **16:9**, **LED 32:9**, **4:3**, or **9:16**.
3. Set **Max lines for this shape** so tall verses split cleanly on wide screens.
4. Set **Safe area margins** for **Left**, **Top**, **Right**, and **Bottom**.
5. Switch **Preview shape** to check your layout at each width.
6. Select **Save template**.

> [!NOTE]
> Use **Use for songs**, **Use for scripture**, or **Assign to selected screen** when your shape rules are ready.
