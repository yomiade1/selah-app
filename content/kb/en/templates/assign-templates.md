# Assign default and screen templates

One service can send big lyrics to the main screen, lower-third words to a stream, and notes to the stage.

## Steps

1. Open **Templates** and select a saved template from the list.
2. Select **Use for songs** to make it your default song template.
3. Select **Use for scripture** to make it your default Bible template.
4. Pick a screen under **Assign to screen**, then select **Assign to selected screen**.

> [!NOTE]
> You can also pick another template for a single song or Bible passage inside the editor.
