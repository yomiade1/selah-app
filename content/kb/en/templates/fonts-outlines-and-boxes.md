# Set fonts, outlines, and card boxes

Every text part on a slide has its own slot. Style body words, titles, references, chords, second-language lines, and credits separately.

## Steps

1. Open **Templates** and select the template you want to edit.
2. Pick a slot from **Text role**.
3. Turn **Show on slide** on or off for that slot.
4. Set **Font**, **Weight**, **Size (pt)**, and **Smallest size (pt)**.
5. Set **Text colour**, **Line height**, **Letter spacing**, **Across**, and **Up and down**.
6. Turn on **Outline**, **Drop shadow**, or **Card box** to stand out over bright clips.
7. Turn on **Full width** when you want a banner across the bottom.
8. Select **Save template**.

> [!TIP]
> Keep at least strong dark **Drop shadow** or a dark **Card box** over bright video backdrops.
