# What a template is

A template is the shared style for words on slides. It picks fonts, sizes, colours, and safe room from screen edges.

## Why it helps

You set a look once and reuse it everywhere. Songs and verses follow the same rule across big rooms and live streams.

## An example

A church uses large centred words in the main room and neat lower-third lines on the online stream.
