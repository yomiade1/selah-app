# Starter looks reference

Select any built-in look under **Starter looks** to load a clean base style.

| Look | Best for | Key traits |
|---|---|---|
| **Sanctuary lyrics** | Main room screens | Bold clean sans type, soft drop shadow, and wide safe margins |
| **Warm serif** | Hymns and readings | Calm serif headings, gentle spacing, and warm off-white ink |
| **Stream lower third** | Online stream feeds | Two-line dock at the bottom inside a dark **Card box** |
| **Stage monitor** | Musicians on platform | High-contrast text, chord symbols above lyrics, and next-line notes |

> [!TIP]
> Use **Duplicate** if you want to test a look without changing your live style.
