# Undo and redo changes

Selah keeps an operation history for songs, media entries, and tags. You can step back inside one song or across your whole library.

## Steps

1. Open **Library** or **Song editor**.
2. Select **Undo** in the action bar to reverse your last saved change.
3. Select **Redo** to put the undone change back.

> [!NOTE]
> Saving a brand new edit clears any redo steps that were waiting ahead of it.

## When nothing is left to undo

Every earlier edit in this scope is already back where it started. You can close the alert and keep working.
