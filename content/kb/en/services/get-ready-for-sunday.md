# Check a service before it starts

Selah can check a service for you before people arrive.

## Steps

1. Select **Services** and pick the service.
2. Select **Check the service**.
3. Read the list under **Ready for Sunday?**:
   - **Fix this** means the service won't run well. Fix it first.
   - **Check this** means something might look wrong.
   - **Note** is something to know.
4. Select **How to fix** next to any line to see what to do.
5. Select **Check the service** again after you fix things.

## What Selah checks

- Pictures, videos and sound files are there and unchanged.
- Every font is installed.
- The words fit on every screen.
- Songs have credits and your CCLI licence number is set.
- Every screen is connected.
- Nothing flashes too fast.
- The volume limiter is on.

> [!NOTE]
> Backup sync isn't in Selah yet. Until it is, back up your library by hand in **Library**.
