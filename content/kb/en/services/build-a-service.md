# Build a service and assign the team

Use the service planner to put songs, Bible verses, media, slides, and timers in order. Selah adds up each item length to show the finish time.

## Steps

1. Select **Services** in the top bar.
2. Select **New service** or **Inherit from last week** to copy your structure.
3. Enter the **Service name** and **Start time**.
4. Select **Add song**, **Add scripture**, **Add media**, or **Add slides** to build your order.
5. Pick an item in the list to set its **Owner**, **Look**, and **Length (s)**.
6. In **Team roster**, enter a **Volunteer name** and **Role**, then select **Add to team**.
7. Select **Save service** to keep your plan.

> [!TIP]
> Select **Move up** or **Move down** to change the item order at any time.
