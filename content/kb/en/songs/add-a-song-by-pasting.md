# Add a song by pasting

You don't have to build slides by hand when adding a song. Paste your words and Selah detects sections and slides.

## Steps

1. Open **Library**, then stay on **Songs** and select **New song**.
2. Selah opens **Song editor** on the **Paste** step.
3. Pick a format if needed, or leave it on **Detect automatically**.
4. Paste your lyrics or ChordPro text into the box.
5. Select **Detect sections**. Selah splits sections and updates the slides on the right.
6. Select **Save song** when you are happy with the slides.

> [!NOTE]
> Selah saves an unsaved draft while you type, so a crash won't lose your work.
