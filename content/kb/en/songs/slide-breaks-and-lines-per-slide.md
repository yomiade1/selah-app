# Control slide breaks and lines

Selah splits long sections for you using **Lines per slide**. You can also place exact breaks inside a verse.

## Steps

1. Open a song in **Song editor** and select **Sections**.
2. Place the cursor after a line and press [[Alt]]+[[Enter]] or select **Add slide break**.
3. Or select **Layout** in the inspector to change **Lines per slide**.
4. Turn **Title slide** or **Blank slide at end** on or off if needed.
5. Check **16:9**, **LED 32:9**, **Stage**, and **Stream** previews on the right.
6. Select **Save song**.

> [!TIP]
> A blank line between lines also starts a new slide.
