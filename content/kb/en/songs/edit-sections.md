# Edit song sections and slides

Once you paste a song, you can adjust section labels, lyrics, slide breaks, and slide previews before saving.

## Steps

1. In **Song editor**, select **Sections**.
2. Select **Add section** to add a verse, chorus, or bridge.
3. Change any section's name, type, key shortcut, or words.
4. Put a blank line or press [[Alt]]+[[Enter]] where you want a new slide.
5. Check the preview cards on the right across **16:9**, **LED 32:9**, **Stage**, and **Stream**.
6. Select **Save song** to keep your changes.

> [!TIP]
> Turn on **Show chords in preview** to inspect how chords sit above the words.
