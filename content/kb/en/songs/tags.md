# Tag songs and media

Tags let you group songs by topic, season, or speed, such as Hymn, Closing, or Advent. Search matches every tag you add.

## Steps

1. Open a song in **Song editor**.
2. Select **Song** in the inspector on the right.
3. Under **Tags**, type comma-separated tag names.
4. Select **Save song**.
5. To filter by a tag later, type the tag word in **Search library**.

> [!TIP]
> Keep tag names short and consistent across your team, such as Hymn or Call to worship.
