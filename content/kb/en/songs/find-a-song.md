# Find a song in Library

You can find any song by its title, writer, tags, CCLI number, or words in a verse. Selah still matches when you make a small typo.

## Steps

1. Open **Library** and select **Songs**.
2. Type words into **Search library**.
3. Select **Search** to filter the list.
4. Pick a song to preview its sections and slides on the right.
5. Select **Edit song** to change its words, chords, or tags.

> [!TIP]
> Clear the search box and select **Search** to see all songs again.
