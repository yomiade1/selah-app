# Add song credits and CCLI details

Store each song's writer, CCLI number, key, and copyright notice in **Song editor**. Selah places the copyright line where your licence requires.

## Steps

1. Open a song in **Song editor**.
2. Select the **Song** tab in the right inspector.
3. Fill in **Title**, **Author**, **Key**, and **Tempo (BPM)**.
4. Enter **CCLI song number** and **Copyright**, or check **Public domain** if it is public domain.
5. Pick where the notice shows under **Copyright line on screen**.
6. Select **Save song**.

> [!NOTE]
> Public domain hymns can leave **Copyright line on screen** set to **None**.
