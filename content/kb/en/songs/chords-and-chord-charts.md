# Chords and transposition

Selah stores chords above lyrics using brackets like `[G]` or `[C]` and keeps them lined up if you change the key.

## Steps

1. In **Song editor**, look at the right inspector and select **Song** to set credits and CCLI number.
2. Select **Layout** to pick lines per slide or title and copyright slides.
3. Select **Arrange** to set the **Order in service** for verses and choruses.
4. Select **Actions** to use **Transpose -1** or **Transpose +1** by semitones.
5. Select **Save song** when the key and arrangement look right.

> [!TIP]
> Turn on **Prefer flats** under **Actions** if your band prefers flats over sharps.
