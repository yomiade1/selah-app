# Set up the stage display

The stage display is the screen that faces the band and the speaker. The people in the seats never see it.

It can show:

- the song, its key and its sections
- the words on screen now, with chords
- the next slide
- a timer and the clock
- slide notes
- messages from the booth

## Steps

1. In Producer mode, select the **Stage** tab.
2. Pick an **Arrangement**: **Lyrics and chords**, **Lyrics only** or **Speaker**.
3. Under **Show on the stage screen**, tick what you want. The **Stage screen preview** shows where each part goes.
4. Move **Text size** to make the words bigger or smaller.
5. Type a **Layout name**, then select **Save stage layout**.
6. Select **Use on stage screens**. The stage screens change right away.

> [!TIP]
> Make one layout for the band and one for the speaker. Switch between them with a macro.

## What you'll see

The tab says **In use on stage screens** under the layout that's showing now.
