# Show chords on the stage display

Selah can put each chord over the right word on the stage screens. The screens in the room show only the words.

## Before you start

The song needs chords. Add them in the song editor.

## Steps

1. In Producer mode, select the **Stage** tab.
2. Tick **Chords over the words**.
3. Tick **Song key** to show the key at the top.
4. Select **Save stage layout**, then **Use on stage screens**.

> [!NOTE]
> If you change the song's key in the service, the chords on the stage change with it.
