# Look up Bible passages and verses

Selah ships with KJV and WEB offline. Type a reference or search words to turn verses into slides.

## Steps

1. Open **Library**, then select **Bibles**.
2. Choose a translation under **Translation**.
3. Type a reference like `John 3:16-18` and select **Look up passage**.
4. Or type words you remember and select **Find verses**.
5. Adjust **Lines per slide** or **Show verse numbers** under **Scripture slide options** to preview slides.

> [!TIP]
> Selah accepts short book names like `jn 3 16` or `Rom 8:28` in 6 languages.
