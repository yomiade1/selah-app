# Choose scripture slide layout options

Control how many lines appear on each scripture slide and where Selah prints the book, chapter, and verse label.

## Steps

1. Open **Library** and select **Bibles**.
2. Look up a passage under **Passage lookup** and select **Look up passage**.
3. Under **Scripture slide options**, set **Lines per slide**.
4. Choose **Show verse numbers** if you want verse numbers on screen.
5. Pick **None**, **Slide header**, or **Slide footer** under **Reference on slide**.
6. Turn **Title slide** or **Blank slide at end** on or off.

> [!NOTE]
> **Scripture slide preview** updates as soon as you change an option.
