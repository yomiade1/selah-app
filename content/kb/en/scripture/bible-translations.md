# Bundled Bible translations

Selah ships with two public-domain Bibles, KJV and WEB. Both work offline as soon as you open the app.

## Why it helps

You can search verses and build slides even in rooms with no internet. Each Bible stays in its own fast local file inside your library folder.

## Adding more translations

If your church has rights to use another translation in OSIS, USFM, or Zefania files, you can add it from **Bibles**. Pick your active version at the top under **Translation**.
