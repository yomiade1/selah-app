# Search words in the Bible

When you remember a phrase instead of a book and chapter, search across the active Bible translation.

## Steps

1. Open **Library** and select **Bibles**.
2. Choose a translation under **Translation**.
3. Type words from the verse into **Search verses**.
4. Select **Find verses**.
5. Pick a verse from **Matched verses** to preview its slides.

> [!TIP]
> You can also type words into **Quick find** with [[Ctrl]]+[[K]] to jump to matching verses.
