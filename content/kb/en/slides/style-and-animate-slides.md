# Style backgrounds, transitions, and effects

The right side of **Slide editor** shows the item box, backdrop, fade, and effects for the slide.

## Steps

1. Pick an item in **Elements** to set its spot, size, or text.
2. Under **Background**, pick a colour, photo, or clip from your library.
3. Under **Transition**, pick how a slide shows up, such as fade, push, wipe, zoom, or slide.
4. Under **Effects**, pick blur or matte look and select **Add effect**.
5. Watch for any **Flash warning** before staging fast slides.

> [!WARNING]
> Selah stops staging a flash risk slide until you check the box.
