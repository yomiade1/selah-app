# Staged and on-screen previews

Selah shows two live windows in the preview pane: **On screen** shows the room screens now, and **Staged preview** shows the next slide waiting in line.

## Why it helps

You can check layout, chords, and warnings in **Staged preview** before showing anything to the congregation. Flashing slides show a **Flash alert** badge before they go live.

## An example

A producer edits a notice slide in **Slide editor**, selects **Stage**, and checks **NEXT** while a hymn stays live on screen.
