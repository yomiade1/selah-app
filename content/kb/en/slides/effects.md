# Slide effects reference

Select **Add effect** under **Effects** to stack up to eight built-in looks on a layer.

| Effect | What it changes | Common use |
|---|---|---|
| Blur | Softens sharp detail across a layer | Calm busy photos under lyrics |
| Colour adjust | Shifts brightness, contrast, hue, or warmth | Match a bright clip to darker words |
| Vignette | Darkens edges around the frame | Draw eyes to centred song words |
| Background blur | Frosted glass blur behind a shape or box | Readable notice cards on live motion |
| Colour key | Drops out one solid background tone | Clean key for simple graphics |
| Mask | Crops a layer inside a box, rounded box, or oval | Frame speakers or picture inlays |

> [!NOTE]
> Pressing **Clear everything** stops transitions and effects in one frame.
