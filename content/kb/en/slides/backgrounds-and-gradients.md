# Set slide backgrounds, photos, and gradients

Every slide has a base backdrop layer that sits behind your words and shapes.

## Steps

1. Open **Slide editor** and pick a slide in **Slide deck**.
2. Look under **Background** in the panel on the right.
3. Set a base colour, soft blend, or pick an **Image** from **Media**.
4. Adjust **Opacity** or soft blur when words need more contrast.
5. Select **Save deck** to keep your backdrop on the slide.

> [!TIP]
> Put photos into **Media** first so Selah saves them safely inside your library.
