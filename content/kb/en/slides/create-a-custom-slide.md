# Create a custom slide deck

Use **Slide editor** when you need custom layouts, images, announcement cards, clocks, or timers on a slide.

## Steps

1. Open **Slide editor** in the toolbar and name your deck.
2. Add elements with **Text**, **Clock**, **Timer**, **Shape**, **Image**, or **Video**.
3. Select two or more items, or choose **Select all**, then select **Center** or **Group**.
4. Use **Forward** or **Backward** to change which element sits on top.
5. Select **Save deck** when your slide looks right.
6. Select **Stage** to check your slide in the next preview pane.

> [!TIP]
> Turn on **Lock position** on background shapes so align tools skip locked items.
