# Video backgrounds and looping

Motion loops sit behind words and play quietly at the frame rate of your clip.

## Steps

1. Open **Library**, select **Media**, then choose **Import media** to add your clip.
2. Check if the clip shows a **Flash alert** badge before using it live.
3. Open **Slide editor** and pick **Video** under **Background**.
4. Pick your clip and set **Opacity** if you need darker words.
5. Select **Save deck**, then choose **Stage** to check the **Staged preview**.

> [!NOTE]
> Selah pre-opens the next loop lap early so looping clips keep steady motion.
