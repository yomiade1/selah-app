# Slide transitions reference

Pick a style under **Transition** to control how one slide gives way to the next.

| Transition | How it moves | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Cut | Switches on the next frame | Fast lyric beats and timing shifts |
| Fade | Cross-blends smoothly over time | Worship songs and Scripture |
| Dissolve | Soft grain blend between slides | Photo stories and calm talks |
| Fade through black | Dims to black, then rises cleanly | Topic shifts between service parts |
| Push | Pushes the old slide aside | Announcements and step lists |
| Wipe | Reveals the new slide across an edge | Clear split changes between cards |
| Zoom | Scales in gently toward the screen | Opening welcomes and title cards |
| Slide | Glides in over the current slide | Side panels and layered cards |

> [!TIP]
> Use **Repeat cadence (ms)** carefully. Fast repeating fades over 3 per second raise a **Flash warning**.
