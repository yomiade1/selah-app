# Add live clocks and countdown timers

Custom slides can show the room time or count down to service start on the second.

## Steps

1. Open **Slide editor** and select a pre-service or notice slide.
2. Choose **Clock** to add the local clock, or **Timer** for a timer.
3. For a clock, turn **Show seconds** or **24-hour clock** on or off.
4. For a timer, enter **Countdown (seconds)** such as 300 for five minutes.
5. Turn on **Count up** if you want the timer to count forward.
6. Edit **Words** if you want extra words around `{clock}` or `{timer}`.
7. Select **Save deck**.

> [!NOTE]
> You can place `{clock}` or `{timer}` inside **Words** on a clock or timer item.
