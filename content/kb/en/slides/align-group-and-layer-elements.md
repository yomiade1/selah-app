# Align, group, and lock slide elements

Use layout tools in **Slide editor** to snap elements cleanly and keep card layers organized.

## Steps

1. Open your slide in **Slide editor**.
2. Pick items in **Elements**, or select **Select all**.
3. Choose **Left**, **Center**, **Right**, **Top**, **Middle**, or **Bottom** to line them up.
4. Select **Group** to move items together, or **Ungroup** later to split them.
5. Select **Forward** or **Backward** to change front-to-back order.
6. Turn on **Lock position** on items you do not want moved.
7. Set exact **X**, **Y**, **Width**, and **Height** values in **Element** when needed.
8. Select **Save deck** when finished.

> [!TIP]
> Locked items show a **Locked** badge and stay safe when you align other elements.
