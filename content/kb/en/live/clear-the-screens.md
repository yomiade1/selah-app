# Clear the screens

You can clear everything at once, or clear one part at a time.

## Clear everything

1. Press F12, or select **Clear everything**.
2. Or hold Esc for 1 second.

The screens clear right away. Selah doesn't ask first, because speed matters.

## Clear one part

- Press F10, or select **Clear text**, to hide the words. The background stays.
- Press F11, or select **Clear background**, to hide the background.
- Press F9, or select **Show logo**, to show your logo. Press F9 again to hide it.
- In **Layers**, select **Clear** next to any layer.

> [!NOTE]
> **Clear everything** works even when the booth is locked.
