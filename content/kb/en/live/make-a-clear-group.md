# Make a clear group

A clear group clears the layers you pick with one tile. For example, it can clear the words and the prop but keep the background.

## Steps

1. In **Live**, select the **Set up tiles** tab.
2. In **What to make**, choose **Clear group**.
3. Type a **Name**, like "Words and props".
4. Under **Layers to clear**, tick each layer to clear.
5. Tick **Stop audio**, **Stop timers**, or **Hide the logo** if you need them.
6. Select **Save**.

## Use it

Select the clear group's tile in **Looks, timers and macros**.

> [!NOTE]
> To clear every screen at once, press F12. You don't need a clear group for that.
