# Arrange the Producer workspace

You can change the size of each pane in Producer mode.

## Steps

1. Drag the edge between two panes to make one bigger.
2. Or pick a layout in **Layout**: **Standard**, **Wide slides**, or **Program focus**.
3. Select **Save layout**. Selah opens Producer mode this way next time.

> [!NOTE]
> The Clear and Next buttons always stay at the bottom. Layouts never hide them.
