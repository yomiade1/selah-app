# Clear the screens in an emergency

If the wrong thing is on screen, clear it first. You can fix it after.

## Steps

1. Press F12.
2. Or hold Esc for 1 second.
3. Or select **Clear everything**.

Every screen clears right away. Selah doesn't ask first.

## Why it always works

- It works when the booth is locked.
- It works while you practise.
- It works even if part of Selah is busy, because it takes its own fast path to the screens.

## Put things back

1. Take a breath.
2. Choose the next slide you want.
3. Press Space or select it to show it.
