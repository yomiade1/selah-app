# Run a service in Producer mode

Producer mode puts your whole service on one screen. You see the service order, the slides, and the **Program** picture of what people see.

## Steps

1. Select **Live** in the top bar.
2. Pick your service in **Service to run**.
3. Select **Load service**. The first item shows on the screens.
4. Press Space to show the next slide. Press the left arrow to go back.
5. Select any slide to show it now.
6. Select an item in **Service order** to jump to it.

> [!TIP]
> In **Services**, select **Go live** to load the service and open Producer mode in one step.

## What you see

- **Service order** shows LIVE next to the item on screen, and NEXT next to the item after it.
- **Slides** shows each slide in the live item. The slide on screen is marked LIVE.
- **Program** shows what is on the main screen.
- The bottom bar shows each screen. **Working** means it is fine.
