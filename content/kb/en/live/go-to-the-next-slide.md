# Go to the next slide

You can move through slides with the keyboard or with the buttons at the bottom.

## Steps

1. Press Space, or select **Next**, to show the next slide.
2. Press the left arrow, or select **Previous**, to go back one slide.
3. Press Shift and the right arrow, or select **Next item**, to start the next item.
4. Press a number key to jump to that verse. For example, press 2 for verse 2.

> [!TIP]
> Showed the wrong slide? Press Ctrl+Z to put the last slide back. On a Mac, press Cmd+Z.
