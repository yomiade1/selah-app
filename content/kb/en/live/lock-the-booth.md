# Lock the booth

Booth lock helps when you step away, or when the booth is busy. It stops accidental changes.

## Steps

1. Select **Booth lock**, or press Ctrl+L. On a Mac, press Cmd+L.
2. A banner says the booth is locked.
3. To unlock, select **Booth lock** again, or press Ctrl+L.

## What still works

- **Next** and **Previous**
- **Clear everything**, **Clear text**, and **Clear background**

Everything else waits until you unlock.
