# Show a song on the screens

You can show any song that is in today's service. You only need to pick the song and a slide.

## Steps

1. At the top of the screen, find the list of items in the service. Select the song.
2. Under **All slides in this song**, select the first slide. It shows on the screens with a red border and the tag **On screen now**.
3. To show the next slide, press [[Space]] or select **Next slide**.
4. To go back one slide, press [[Left arrow]] or select **Back**.
5. When the song ends, select **Next song**.

> [!TIP]
> The slide with the green border comes next. Look at it before you press [[Space]].

## If the words do not show

- Look at the dot next to **All screens working**. If it is not green, ask the booth for help.
- If you hid the words, select **Hide words** again. They will come back.
- Still stuck? Select **Message the booth**. The person in the booth will help you.

## In Producer mode

The steps are the same. The service list is on the left side of the screen instead of the top.
