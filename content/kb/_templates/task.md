# Do something (verb first)

Say what this does for the reader in 1 or 2 short sentences.

## Steps

1. Say where, then what. "In **Today's service**, select the song."
2. One action per step. Use the exact words on the screen, in bold.
3. No more than 10 steps. Split longer tasks into 2 articles.

> [!TIP]
> One helpful idea, if there is one.

## If it doesn't work

- The most likely fix first.
